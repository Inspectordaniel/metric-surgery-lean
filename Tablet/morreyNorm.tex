\begin{definition}[Morrey norm of a measure]
  Let $E$ be a metric space equipped with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let $\mu$ be a Borel
  measure on $E$. The Morrey norm of $\mu$ is
  \[
    \Morrey{\mu} = \sup_{r>0,\, x \in E} \frac{\mu(B_r(x))}{r}
    ,
  \]
  where $B_r(x)$ is the closed ball of radius $r$ about $x$ (matching the condition
  $d(x,\gamma(t)) \le r$ used in the paper's ball-growth display, eq.~(1.1)), and the supremum is
  taken in $[0,\infty]$ -- exactly paper eq.~(2.3) (paper.tex line 474). For a curve $\gamma$, the
  Morrey norm $\Morrey{\gamma}$ of eq.~(1.1) (paper.tex lines 259--262) is the Morrey norm
  $\Morrey{\mu_\gamma}$ of its measure $\mu_\gamma$, per paper eq.~(2.3)/line 478; downstream nodes
  write this out inline as $\Morrey{\mu_\gamma}$ rather than introducing a separate alias
  definition.
\end{definition}
