\begin{helper}[Length is measurable]
  Let $E$ be a metric space equipped with a $\sigma$-algebra. Then $\gamma \mapsto \length(\gamma)$
  is a measurable map $\Theta(E) \to \mathbb{R}$, with respect to the evaluation $\sigma$-algebra on
  $\Theta(E)$ (\noderef{CurveMeasurableSpace}).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
  By definition (\noderef{CurveMeasurableSpace}), $\Theta(E)$ carries the pullback $\sigma$-algebra
  along $\gamma \mapsto (\length(\gamma),\gamma)$, so this evaluation map is measurable by the
  universal property of the pullback $\sigma$-algebra (a map into a pullback-equipped space is
  measurable with respect to the pullback exactly when the composite with the defining map is
  measurable, applied here to the identity composite). Composing with the (measurable) first
  coordinate projection $\mathbb{R} \times (\mathbb{R}\to E) \to \mathbb{R}$ gives that
  $\gamma \mapsto \length(\gamma)$ is measurable.
\end{proof}
