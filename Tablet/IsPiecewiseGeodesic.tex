\begin{definition}[Piecewise geodesic curve, $\Xgeo$]
  Let $E$ be a metric space and $\gamma \in \Theta(E)$ (\noderef{Curve}). We say $\gamma$
  \emph{is piecewise geodesic}, written $\mathrm{IsPiecewiseGeodesic}(\gamma)$, when
  $\exists k \in \mathbb{N},\ \mathrm{IsPiecewiseGeodesicWith}(\gamma,k)$
  (\noderef{IsPiecewiseGeodesicWith}). This is exactly the paper's set $\Xgeo \subset \Theta(E)$ of
  piecewise geodesic curves (paper.tex line 537): $\gamma \in \Xgeo$ iff $\gamma$ admits a
  resolution into geodesic edges for some number of pieces $k$.
\end{definition}
